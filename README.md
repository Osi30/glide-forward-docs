# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Glide Forward** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed as an independent project, handles user information. 

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. **Glide Forward** is designed as a fully offline, stand-alone arcade game that does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Glide Forward** does **NOT** collect, request, transmit, or share any personal data or device information from users. 

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, or hardware identifiers.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not integrate any third-party SDKs, tracking tools, or analytics software (such as Google Analytics or Firebase).
- **No Internet Required:** The game operates entirely offline and does not establish network connections.

---

## 2. Local Data Storage (`PlayerPrefs`)

To save your game progress (such as unlocked levels or settings), **Glide Forward** utilizes Unity's local `PlayerPrefs` mechanism. 

- This data is stored **locally on your device** only.
- It is never transmitted to any external server or shared with any third party.
- Clearing the application data or uninstalling the game will remove this saved progress completely.

---

## 3. Advertising and Purchases

- **No Ads:** **Glide Forward** contains zero advertisements (no banners, interstitials, or rewarded ads).
- **No In-App Purchases (IAP):** The game is completely free and contains no paid features or microtransactions.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

While **Glide Forward** contains no harmful, violent, or explicit content, the game is primary targeted at general audiences aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
